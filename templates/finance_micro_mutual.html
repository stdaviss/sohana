About
Sohana Micro Mutual Fund Terminal — models a ROSCA-style circle where fixed member contributions are pooled every cycle and deployed into an investment product rather than rotated as an individual payout. Returns use a time-weighted growing-annuity engine (each cycle's pot compounds from its own contribution date), not a naive lump-sum estimate.
