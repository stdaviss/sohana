Scenarios to try
  1. MoMo deposit to a member: credited at once.
  2. Same provider reference again: recorded as a duplicate, no money moves.
  3. Bank transfer with the member's deposit reference: credited.
  4. Bank transfer with a wrong reference: lands in suspense; match it in Deposits & suspense.
  5. Card deposit, then the member tries to withdraw it: refused for 14 days.
  6. Card deposit, the member spends part, then a chargeback: the rest is taken back, the spent part is a recorded loss.
  7. A withdrawal: approve, mark paid with a reference; or reject it and watch the money return.
  8. Fast-forward a circle past its due date: unpaid members are marked missed and the pot pays out what was collected.
