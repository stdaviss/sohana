What is the NCS?
What Sohana Verify shows: The holder's name, handle, current NCS score, tier, cycles completed, reliability rate, and tenure — only public, holder-consented information. What it does not show: Wallet balances, transaction history, contact details, KYC documents, or any private data. Every verification is logged for the passport holder's records.
